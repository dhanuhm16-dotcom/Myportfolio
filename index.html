Hardware & Software Prototype

Automatic Petrol Pump System

Problem Statement & Vision

Traditional fuel dispensing relies on constant manual coordination, which can lead to measurement discrepancies, bottlenecks during peak hours, and safety oversights. This project aimed to design a self-regulating, sensor-monitored automated fuel distribution module.

Technical Architecture & Implementation

  • Automated Dispensing Routine: Developed algorithmic decision trees in Python and C to calibrate volume delivery per transaction.
  • Sensor Feedback Loop: Integrated flow sensors and proximity indicators to regulate valve triggering and prevent spills or overfills.
  • Safety & Override Protocols: Programmed fail-safe cut-offs to safely shut down fuel transmission in case of sensor anomalies.
  • Development Duration: 3 months of rigorous hardware breadboarding, logic scripting, and prototype testing.
